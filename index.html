ROOTED IN YOUR NEIGHBORHOOD

Lawn care that knows
West Lafayette.

From established lawns near campus to family yards across West Lafayette, Indiana’s seasons keep grass on its toes. Cool-season favorites like Kentucky bluegrass and tall fescue thrive in the milder weather of spring and fall.

Mowing typically begins with spring green-up and continues into fall, often from April through October depending on the year. A flexible schedule follows your grass’s growth, with more frequent cuts in active periods and fewer during summer heat or dry spells.

Warm, humid summers and cold winters call for thoughtful care. Keep grass a little taller during hot weather, avoid taking off more than one-third of the blade at once, and consider late-summer or early-fall overseeding to help thin spots recover.

Seasonal guidance informed by Purdue Extension. Timing varies with weather and your lawn.
